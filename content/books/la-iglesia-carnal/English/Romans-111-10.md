11:1  I ask THEN,                                      [main: inference]
          did God reject his people?                  [content: what Paul asks]

      May it never be!                                [independent answer]
    
      FOR I ALSO am an Israelite,                     [main: supporting reason]
      a descendant of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.


11:2  God didn’t reject his people,                    [main assertion]
          WHICH he foreknew.                          [relative: describes “people”]

      OR don’t you know                               [main rhetorical question]
          WHAT the Scripture says about Elijah,       [content: what they know]
              HOW he pleads with God against Israel:  [content: explains what Scripture says]


11:3              “Lord, they have killed your prophets;  [quotation content]

                  they have broken down your altars;     [coordinate accusation]
    
                  AND I am left alone;                   [coordinate statement]
    
                  AND they seek my life.”                [coordinate statement]


11:4  BUT how does God answer him?                    [main: contrasting response]

      “I have reserved for myself seven thousand men, [main clause in God’s answer]
          WHO have not bowed the knee to Baal.”       [relative: describes the men]


11:5  EVEN SO THEN at this present time ALSO
      there is a remnant
      according to the election of grace.             [main: application of Elijah’s example]


11:6      AND IF [it is] by grace,                    [dependent condition]
      THEN [it is] no longer of works;                 [main: conclusion of condition]
          OTHERWISE grace is no longer grace.         [dependent explanation]


11:7  What THEN?                                      [elliptical question: inference]

          THAT WHICH Israel seeks for,                [relative: identifies what was sought]
      THAT he didn’t obtain;                          [main: resumes the preceding relative]
    
      BUT the chosen ones obtained it;                [coordinate contrast]
    
      AND the rest were hardened,                     [coordinate contrasting outcome]


11:8      ACCORDING AS it is written,                  [dependent: scriptural correspondence]
              “God gave them a spirit of stupor,
               eyes                                   [object phrase]
                  THAT they should not see,           [dependent infinitive: modifies “eyes”]
               AND ears                               [coordinate object phrase]
                  THAT they should not hear,          [dependent infinitive: modifies “ears”]
               to this very day.”                     [time phrase within “God gave …”]


11:9  [AND] David says,                                [main: additional scriptural witness]
          “Let their table be made a snare,
           AND a trap,
           a stumbling block,
           AND a retribution to them.                 [one quoted clause; coordinated phrases]


11:10     Let their eyes be darkened,                  [coordinate quoted clause]
              THAT they may not see.                  [dependent infinitive: purpose/result]

          [AND] always keep their backs bent.”        [coordinate quoted clause]
