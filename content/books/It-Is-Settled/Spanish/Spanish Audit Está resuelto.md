# Spanish Audit: Está resuelto

Nov 5, 2026 · @bjwry

## Summary

The Spanish is in good shape: a handful of real errors, a cluster of English calques, and several consistency problems with the LPF quotes.

- Spelling, accents (*aun así*, *solo*, *sí importa*), «» quotation marks, ellipses and dialogue dashes are all correct.
- Dialect is consistent: Latin American, *tú* to the reader, *ustedes* in the quotes.
- I had no copy of LPF, so I flagged only internal inconsistencies in the quotes, not wrong quotes.

## Grammar and punctuation errors

The thesis line needs a comma, and several tense, mood and point-of-view slips need fixing.

- **Thesis line, Propósito:** "Lo que Dios ya resolvió no tenemos que volver a resolverlo nosotros." needs a comma after *resolvió*, because the fronted object is picked up again by *-lo*. This is the book's key sentence, so it should be perfect.
- **Mov. 5, "Una nueva posición":** "No permanece firme en la gracia porque **aprendió** a mantenerse allí" should be "porque **haya aprendido**". The two lines before it use the subjunctive (*haya resuelto*, *haya logrado*).
- **Mov. 9:** "no **equivalía** al rechazo… no **equivalía** a la cancelación… no **equivale** a dar marcha atrás". The tense changes mid-series; make all three the same.
- **Gender agreement, Mov. 2:** *las personas* is followed by *los entregó / los entrega / entregarlos* but also by "fueron **entregadas**". The masculine fits the quotation, but the prose is mixed. Pick one pattern.
- **Mov. 2, end:** "lo profundo que **llega** el problema" should be "lo profundo que **es** el problema" or "hasta dónde llega".
- **Epílogo:** "la base **de que** se reciban" should be "la base **para que** se reciban".
- **Epílogo, point of view:** it starts in the third person ("Pablo les pide… **su** vida", "**Son** miembros", "**Su** servicio") and then switches to "**Nuestro** servicio". Choose one.
- **Mov. 6, "Confiar en lo que es real":** "La Escritura no habla de limitarnos a estar presentes." is unclear and I couldn't work out the intended meaning. Please rewrite it.

**Minor items**

- "Entonces ¿por qué peco?" needs a comma after *Entonces*.
- "Tal vez no **faltas**" is better as *faltes*.
- "analizando **al** mundo" should drop the *a*.
- "condenarse a uno mismo" should be *a sí mismo*.
- "a través de correcciones" is better as *mediante*.
- "no sustituye hacer lo que Dios exige" reads better as *no sustituye el hacer…* or *no equivale a hacer…*.
- "El intercambio cambia aquello que adora" is clumsy: *cambiar* repeats and *adora* has no clear subject. Try "lo que se adora".

## English calques

Eight phrases read as translated English; "relación justa" is the most frequent.

| Phrase in the book | Problem | Suggested fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "ponerse en una relación justa con Dios" (about 5 times) | Mirrors "right relationship" | *estar bien con Dios*, *en paz con Dios*, *ser justo ante Dios* |
| "Hacía compromisos" | Mirrors "made commitments" | *Me comprometía* |
| "Lo han llenado con algo más"; "poner algo más en su lugar" (Mov. 2, twice) | "something else" as *algo más* | *otra cosa* |
| "Lo hace personal" | Mirrors "makes it personal" | *Se lo aplica directamente* |
| "ya no se aborda mediante las obras" | *abordar* here means "tackle" | *ya no se basa en*, *ya no pasa por* |
| "marca una diferencia real" | Mirrors "makes a real difference" | *supone*, *cambia las cosas* |
| "manejarlo" (the sin) | Mirrors "manage it" | *dominarlo*, *someterlo* |
| "contar con" (Mov. 6 and Epílogo) | In Spanish it means "rely on", but Rom 6:11 says *considérense* (reckon) | Where the sense is "reckon": *considerar*, *tener por*, *dar por* |

## Consistency with the quoted text

The quotes mix conventions for the divine name, pronouns and references; check each against LPF.

- **Divine name:** *Yahvé* (Deut 29:29) but *SEÑOR* (Is 55:8).
- **Pronoun form:** Is 55:8 has *vuestros*, while every other quote uses *ustedes*.
- **Capitalized pronouns:** *Su alma* (epigraph), *Tus testimonios* and *Tu palabra… tu justicia* in the same verse (Sal 119:160), against lowercase everywhere else.
- **Rom 8:2:** "te ha librado" (check against LPF, since *me* is the common reading).
- **Old self:** the prose says "viejo ser" but the quote of Rom 6:6 says "viejo hombre".
- **Jesucristo / Jesús Cristo:** the prose uses *Jesucristo* and the quotes use *Jesús Cristo*. This is probably intentional, but decide on purpose.
- **Unattributed inline quotes:** Rom 2:1, 2:21, 3:1 ("Mucho, en toda manera") and 4:17 have no reference, unlike the block quotes.
- **Epílogo references:** it uses bare "(3:24)" and "(14:4)" while the rest of the book writes "Romanos 3:24".
- **Headings:** "Movimiento 4" appears twice, the second as "Continuación".

## Repetition

Repetition is the issue a reader will notice most; these cuts are editorial, so I left them to you.

- "Cristo no es solo una inspiración, un ejemplo que imitar o un símbolo religioso. En él se encuentra la redención." appears word for word twice in Mov. 4.
- Rom 5:9–10 are quoted twice in Mov. 5, in "Una nueva posición" and again in "Una situación nueva".
- In Mov. 7, Rom 7:4, 7:6, Rom 5:2 and Tito 2:11–12 each appear in two consecutive sections.
- The closing refrains of Mov. 3 ("toda boca queda cerrada", "quien juzga mientras practica las mismas cosas") recur five or more times.
- The Preludio has "Pablo empieza con lo que ocurrió antes… Pablo empieza antes de la lista de pecados" in back-to-back sentences.

## Title note and next step

Keep *Está resuelto*: *resuelto* means "solved", while "settled" is closer to "established", and that fits the thesis line (*ya resolvió*) as long as the book keeps using *resolver* and not *decidir* or *zanjar*.
