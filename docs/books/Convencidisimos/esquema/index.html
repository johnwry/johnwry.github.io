Apertura

1. Convencidísimo — El que predica y el que se lo cree.

The confession. Dominion of the room, the phrases, the adopted sincerity, the theology never once questioned. The collapse into feeling communion, feeling spiritual, powerless. The one-way relationship. The lookup scene — where you lose. Ends turning outward: muy bien, encendamos la luz. That last line is the door into Part I, which is why the light image doesn’t need a chapter of its own.

Nota antes de empezar (3 pages, not a chapter). The editorial liberty, stated plainly: we’re regrouping what the letter says, changing the order of presentation and not the sense of what’s presented. Also the method — we examine sentences, not hearts. Sentences are audible. Hearts flatter.

Parte I — Eso decimos

Six chapters, one lamp each, titled with the claim so the reader agrees with the title before he regrets it.

I.1 — «Tenemos comunión con él» · 1:6-7 The partnership the other party has no record of. Koinonía, the RVR60 column, fuera de comunión taken apart. Puerta: 1:7 — the light turns out to produce comunión unos con otros. It was the living room.

I.2 — «No tenemos pecado» · 1:8, 1:10 Both denials, plus the vending machine: 1:9 as procedure. The alternating grid. El pecado no apaga la luz; la versión, sí. Puerta: 1:9 y 2:1-2 — él es fiel, and there is an Abogado. The answer is the next verse.

I.3 — «Yo le conozco» · 2:3-5 Knowledge that never reached the hands. Puerta: 2:5.

I.4 — «Permanezco en él» · 2:6 The devotional claim nobody defines. Note the anomaly: this is the one declaration the letter doesn’t refute — it states the obligation instead. Puerta: 2:8 — la luz verdadera ya alumbra. Already on.

I.5 — «Estoy en la luz» · 2:9-11 Hatred alongside spiritual confidence. Blinded eyes, full stride, the coffee table. The loneliness of the man who cannot be met because what people meet is the version. Puerta: 2:10, y 2:12 — os escribo… porque vuestros pecados os han sido perdonados.

I.6 — «Yo amo a Dios» · 4:20-21 The invisible God is the perfect partner for self-deception: he can be scripted, and he never contradicts. The visible brother talks back. That’s why the letter uses him as the instrument. Puerta: 4:19, which stands before the accusation — the answer was already in the room when the charge was filed.

Parte II — Por qué funciona

Argument, not exposition. It should say so and not dress itself in proof texts.

II.1 — La credencial. The evidence is never erased, only relabeled: discernment, firm convictions, necessary boundaries, prudence, doctrinal maturity. Darkness gets a Christian name tag and a seat on a committee.

II.2 — Heredamos frases. Where the labels come from. Nobody adopted the doctrine; they adopted the expression. Why sincerity measures grip and not ground. The five questions, with ¿qué me permite no hacer? doing the cutting.

II.3 — Dominio del salón. How a congregation maintains a shared version, and why leaving is expensive in a currency that isn’t argument. Your own line is the title.

Parte III — Esto ya era verdad

Indicatives. No numbered program.

III.1 — Dios es luz · 1:5, 1:7 — and it was never an interrogation lamp. III.2 — Tenemos Abogado · 2:1-2 — why the first half of the book could afford to be that hard. III.3 — Mayor que nuestro corazón · 3:19-20 — for the reader whose heart has now overcorrected into accusing everything. Structurally necessary after six chapters of exposure. III.4 — Él nos amó primero · 4:9-10, 19 — the end of self-evaluation as a competitive sport. III.5 — Para que sepáis · 5:11-13 — the letter’s stated purpose is knowing, not doubting.

Cierre

Convencidísimos — Y esta vez, el que habla no soy yo.

Carries 5:20-21. Guardaos de los ídolos stops being an abrupt ending: an idol is a god who cannot participate — he receives and never answers, which is the whole book in one noun. And the admission that this book will be inherited as phrases too, which is why the reader leaves holding the test rather than the verdicts.
